# FF Designer
### Design at the speed of thought.

---

## ✦ Generate. Refine. Ship.

**Describe it. See it instantly.**
Type a prompt and watch your UI come to life in real time. FF Designer streams your design as it generates — no waiting, no blank screens. Just ideas becoming interfaces.

**Pick your direction.**
Every generation gives you multiple distinct design directions to choose from, presented in a beautiful full-screen preview. Not quite right? Refine any variant with a follow-up prompt until it's exactly what you envisioned.

**Designed for every screen.**
Switch between phone, tablet, and desktop presets in one click. Generate screens, individual components, or explore variations of what you already have.

**Bring your inspiration.**
Attach screenshots, sketches, or reference images alongside your prompt. FF Designer understands context — visual and written.

---

## ✦ A Canvas That Keeps Up With You

**Infinite room to think.**
Pan, zoom, and spread your work across an infinite canvas. Navigate massive storyboards effortlessly with the minimap — a live thumbnail overview always within reach.

**Your work, always where you left it.**
Your viewport and selections are saved directly in the URL. Share a link and your collaborators land exactly where you want them.

---

## ✦ Edit Everything, Right There

**Click and type. That's it.**
Edit any text directly on the canvas. No modals, no detours — just click and start typing.

**A properties panel that knows what you need.**
The right panel adapts to whatever you've selected. Editing a button? See button properties. Editing a text style? See typography controls. Clean, contextual, and never overwhelming.

**Color the way you think.**
A full-featured color picker with hex/RGB input, an eyedropper for sampling anything on screen, and a recent colors tray so your palette is always close.

**Every icon, at your fingertips.**
Search and insert from the full Material icons library without ever leaving your canvas.

---

## ✦ Design Tokens, Built In

**A theme that flows through everything.**
Edit your colors, typography, spacing, radii, and shadows in one place — and watch every screen update live before you commit. This isn't a stylesheet. It's your entire design language, editable in real time.

**Scale with a slider.**
Adjust your spacing scale, type scale, or border radius system with a single drag. See the ripple effect across your whole design instantly.

**Start from presets. Make it yours.**
Jump-start with theme presets, then fine-tune to match your brand. Reset to the original AI-generated theme anytime.

---

## ✦ Storyboard Like a Pro

**All your screens, one view.**
The frames panel gives you a bird's-eye view of your entire flow. Reorder, rename, duplicate, or delete screens — all from a clean, animated sidebar.

**Layers you can actually navigate.**
A full hierarchical layer tree lets you drill into any part of your design. Drag to reorder. Drag to reparent. Click to select — the canvas snaps right to it.

**Undo anything. Everything.**
A 100-step undo history with human-readable labels like "Changed button color" or "Moved card" means you always know what you're stepping back through.

---

## ✦ Share, Export, and Collaborate

**Share your storyboard in one click.**
Generate a public link to your canvas. Anyone can view, explore, and remix your work — no account required.

**Export to FlutterFlow.**
Go straight from design to production. Export your screens directly into FlutterFlow and start building.

**Download your work, your way.**
Capture individual screens as PNGs or bundle your full storyboard into a ZIP with documentation included.

---

## ✦ Feels Right Everywhere

**Dark mode, light mode, your mode.**
FF Designer follows your system preference automatically, or you can set it manually. It always looks at home.

**Optimized for mobile too.**
On smaller screens, FF Designer shifts into a focused mobile experience — swipe between frames, prompt from the bottom bar, and edit themes full-screen. The full power of the tool, in the palm of your hand.

**Built for Mac.**
Native macOS touches — transparent titlebars, dock icon sync with light/dark mode — because details matter.
